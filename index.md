# GR Link — Ricoh GR companion app for iPhone

> GR Link is a free, independent iOS companion for Ricoh GR II through GR IV cameras. It connects directly to a camera over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi for remote shooting, live view, photo transfer, geotagging, and Image Control recipe management.

GR Link requires iOS 17.2 or later. It is available from the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gr-link/id6757835191). The app does not upload photos, recipes, camera credentials, or location data to a server operated by GR Link. Optional recipe and preference sync uses the user's private iCloud container.

## Supported cameras

- Ricoh GR II
- Ricoh GR III and GR III HDF
- Ricoh GR IIIx and GR IIIx HDF
- Ricoh GR IV and GR IV HDF
- Ricoh GR IV Monochrome

Features vary by camera hardware and firmware. See the [camera compatibility guide](https://gr-link.liljackson.org/compatible-cameras/) for the verified differences.

## What GR Link does

### Pair and connect

GR Link pairs with supported Bluetooth-equipped cameras, transitions to the camera's Wi-Fi network when a feature needs it, and reconnects when the camera wakes. GR II uses a direct Wi-Fi setup because it does not provide the same Bluetooth workflow as later models.

### Remote shutter and live view

Use the iPhone as a remote viewfinder, trigger the shutter, tap to focus, pinch to zoom, and adjust supported exposure controls. Available controls depend on the connected camera model and firmware.

### Browse and transfer photos

Browse the camera's SD card, preview JPEG and DNG files, select multiple images, and transfer them to the iPhone. A download queue and Live Activity report ongoing transfer progress.

### Manage Image Control recipes

Create, edit, tag, import, export, and share recipes. Recipes can be associated with compatible camera models and synced through the user's private iCloud account. Supported GR IV models can receive recipes in Custom 1/2/3 slots over Bluetooth.

### Privacy

Camera communication happens directly between the iPhone and camera. GR Link operates no photo-upload or analytics server and includes no advertising or third-party tracking SDK. Read the [privacy policy](https://gr-link.liljackson.org/privacy.html) for permission and storage details.
