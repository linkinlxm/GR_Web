# Ricoh GR remote control and live view on iPhone

GR Link can turn an iPhone into a remote controller and live viewfinder for supported Ricoh GR cameras. The phone communicates directly with the camera; no internet relay or GR Link account is involved.

## Remote features

- Live view streamed from the camera over its local Wi-Fi network.
- Remote shutter release.
- Tap to focus and pinch to zoom where the camera API supports them.
- Shutter speed, aperture, ISO, exposure compensation, and image-effect controls where exposed by the connected model.
- Bluetooth Low Energy shutter operation without a Wi-Fi session on supported models.
- Wake, reconnect, and power controls where provided by the camera firmware.

GR Link detects the connected camera and hides controls that the model does not support. This is important because the GR II, GR III/IIIx families, and GR IV family do not expose identical connection or remote-control capabilities.

## Connection notes

Live view needs the camera's Wi-Fi network even when initial pairing happens over Bluetooth. For a stable session, stay near the camera and avoid connecting a second phone to the same camera network.

See the [camera compatibility guide](https://gr-link.liljackson.org/compatible-cameras/) and [download GR Link from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gr-link/id6757835191).
