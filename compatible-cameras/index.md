# Cameras supported by GR Link

Last verified: August 10, 2026. GR Link requires iOS 17.2 or later.

GR Link supports the Ricoh GR II, GR III, GR III HDF, GR IIIx, GR IIIx HDF, GR IV, GR IV HDF, and GR IV Monochrome. It adapts its interface to the features exposed by each camera and firmware version.

## Compatibility summary

| Camera family | Connection | Gallery and JPEG/DNG transfer | Remote and live view | Recipe support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GR II | Direct camera Wi-Fi | Supported | Camera/firmware-dependent controls | Library, import, export, and sharing |
| GR III / GR III HDF | Bluetooth pairing plus camera Wi-Fi | Supported | Live view and remote controls | Library, import, export, tagging, and iCloud sync |
| GR IIIx / GR IIIx HDF | Bluetooth pairing plus camera Wi-Fi | Supported | Live view and remote controls | Library, import, export, tagging, and iCloud sync |
| GR IV / GR IV HDF | Bluetooth pairing plus camera Wi-Fi | Supported | Live view and remote controls | Library plus Bluetooth push to Custom 1/2/3 where supported |
| GR IV Monochrome | Bluetooth pairing plus camera Wi-Fi | Supported | Model-adapted live view and remote controls | Monochrome-aware recipes; camera-write availability depends on parameters and firmware |

## Why features vary

The cameras do not expose identical Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, remote-control, and Image Control interfaces. GR Link detects the model and shows only relevant actions rather than presenting the same controls for every camera.

Firmware updates can change camera behavior. If a feature does not behave as described, include the exact camera model, firmware version, iOS version, and GR Link version in a message to [support@liljackson.org](mailto:support@liljackson.org?subject=GR%20Link%20Compatibility).
