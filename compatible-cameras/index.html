Compatibility guide · Verified August 10, 2026

Ricoh GR cameras supported by GR Link

GR Link supports GR II through GR IV, including HDF and Monochrome models. The app detects the connected camera and adapts connection, remote, gallery, and recipe features to that model.

Why features vary

GR generations expose different Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, remote-control, and Image Control interfaces. GR Link gates features by model so an unsupported action is not presented as universally available. Camera firmware can also change behavior.

Report a compatibility issue

Send the exact camera model, firmware, iPhone and iOS version, GR Link version, and reproduction steps to support@liljackson.org.
