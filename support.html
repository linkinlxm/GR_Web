Frequently Asked Questions

Which cameras are supported?

GR II, GR III, GR III HDF, GR IIIx, GR IIIx HDF, GR IV, GR IV HDF, and GR IV Monochrome. Individual features are gated per model based on what each camera firmware supports. See the camera compatibility guide for details.

The camera won't pair over Bluetooth.

GR II does not support Bluetooth; use its Wi-Fi connection. For Bluetooth-equipped models, make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone and the camera. Put the camera into pairing mode (see your camera's manual), then tap "Pair" inside GR Link. If the pairing fails, toggle Bluetooth off and on again and retry.

Live view is laggy or disconnects.

Live view uses the camera's Wi-Fi. Stay within a few meters of the camera and make sure no other devices are connected to the camera's Wi-Fi simultaneously. If the connection drops, bring the devices closer and reconnect.

My recipes didn't sync to my other device.

Recipe sync uses iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and make sure iCloud Drive is enabled in iOS Settings. Sync can take a few minutes after a change.

Is GR Link made by Ricoh?

No. GR Link is an independent third-party companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Ricoh Imaging Company, Ltd.
