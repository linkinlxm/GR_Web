More Ways to Use Your GR

Features for Ricoh GR photographers. Availability depends on the camera and firmware; GR II does not support Bluetooth.

Remote Control

Use Wi-Fi live view and adjust supported shutter, aperture, ISO, and exposure controls from your iPhone.

Photo Gallery

Browse, preview, and batch download photos directly from your camera to your iPhone.

Recipes

Import, store, and manage custom image recipes from your photos. Organize your GR looks with ease.

BLE Shutter

Trigger the shutter over Bluetooth Low Energy on supported cameras, without a Wi-Fi connection.

Multi-Camera

Manage multiple cameras across the GR II, GR III, GR IIIx, and GR IV families, with features adapted to each model.

Community Recipes

20 curated image recipes ready to import into GR Link. One file, all the looks.

Download Recipes (.grrecipe)

Open the file with GR Link to import all 20 recipes.

Supported Ricoh GR Cameras

GR Link supports GR II, GR III, GR III HDF, GR IIIx, GR IIIx HDF, GR IV, GR IV HDF, and GR IV Monochrome. Connection and camera-control features adapt to each model and firmware.
